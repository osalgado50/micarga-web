1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on the user's device when they visit a website.

They make it possible to remember information about browsing, keep sessions open, protect security, save preferences and, where permitted, analyse how the site is used.

The web application may also use similar technologies, such as local storage, session identifiers or technologies needed to keep the application working.

3. Cookies used on micarga.es

The website https://micarga.es/ installs no analytics, advertising or tracking cookie until the user accepts it in the cookie panel. If they decline, no Google Analytics resource is loaded and no data is sent to Google.

If analytics is accepted, these cookies from Google Analytics 4 (provider: Google Ireland Limited) are installed:

CookiePurposeDuration
_gaTells visitors apart so we can count how many different people visit the site.2 years
_ga_<ID>Keeps the measurement session state.2 years

If advertising is also accepted, Google Analytics enables the Google Ads campaign measurement signals, which tell us whether an ad led to a visit or a sign-up. If declined, those signals stay off.

To remember the choice we store in the browser itself, in local storage rather than a cookie, the entry micarga-cookies, holding what was accepted and the date. It expires after 12 months, and once expired we ask again. It is technical storage needed to honour the choice itself, and it is not sent to anyone.

The site also loads two external resources, needed to display the page correctly, which install no tracking cookies:

  • Google Fonts (the Outfit and Space Grotesk typefaces), served from fonts.googleapis.com.
  • Font Awesome (icons), served from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.

The application https://app.micarga.es/ uses technical storage needed to keep the user signed in (authentication) and for the progressive web app to work offline. These technologies are strictly necessary to provide the service requested and do not require consent.

4. Types of cookies

4.1. Technical or necessary cookies

Needed for the website or the app to work properly: keeping the session open, authenticating the user, protecting the platform against unauthorised access, remembering essential preferences or managing security. They do not require consent.

4.2. Preference cookies

These remember choices made by the user, such as language or visual settings. Where they are not strictly necessary, they will only be set after consent is obtained.

4.3. Analytics cookies

These provide statistical information about how the site is used. They will not be set without consent where they are not technically necessary.

4.4. Advertising or marketing cookies

These measure campaigns or show personalised advertising. Mi Carga will not set these cookies without prior, informed, specific and revocable consent.

The detail of every cookie installed — provider, purpose and duration — is in section 3 of this same page.

5. Consent

The first time you enter the site a panel appears letting you accept all, reject all o choose by category. Rejecting costs exactly the same as accepting: one click, on an equally visible button.

Until you answer, no non-essential cookie is installed and no Google resource is loaded.

You can change or withdraw the choice at any time from the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it and has no effect on your use of the site.

6. Browser settings

Users can allow, block or delete cookies from their browser settings. Disabling technical cookies may stop parts of the website or the app from working properly.

Information on managing cookies is available in the help pages of the main browsers:

  • Google Chrome: support.google.com/chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox: support.mozilla.org
  • Microsoft Edge: support.microsoft.com
  • Safari: support.apple.com

7. Data protection

Information obtained through cookies that makes it possible to identify a person directly or indirectly may constitute personal data. Such data will be processed in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Spanish Organic Act 3/2018 and the applicable rules on information society services.

Full information on the processing of personal data can be found in the Privacy and data protection policy.

8. Updates

Mi Carga may amend this Cookie Policy when the technologies used, the providers, the purposes or the applicable rules change.
