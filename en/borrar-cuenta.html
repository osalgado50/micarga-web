Delete my account

You can delete your Mi Carga account from here, even if you no longer have the app installed. You can also do it inside the app, under Settings → Delete my account.

What gets deleted

Your profile and sign-in details, your billing details, and the companies, consignors and trailers you have saved. It is final: there is no way to get the account back.

What is kept, and why

The consignment notes you already issued and the invoices are kept, no longer linked to any account. This is not our decision: Spanish tax law and the Commercial Code require invoices to be kept for between 4 and 6 years, and transport control documents have their own retention period. The GDPR expressly provides for this case (article 17.3.b). Once the account is deleted you will no longer be able to download them, so save them first if you need them.

We'll send you a 6-digit code to check the account is yours. Without it we delete nothing.

Account deleted

Your account and your personal data have been deleted. If you need Mi Carga again, you'll have to create a new account from scratch.
