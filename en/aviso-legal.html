6. Digital transport documentation

The platform may allow administrative control documents, consignment notes and other documents relating to transport operations to be produced, depending on the features available at any given time.

Documents are produced from the data provided by the customer. The customer is responsible for:

  • The accuracy of the details of the loader, carrier, consignee, driver, vehicle, goods, route and any other information.

  • Handing the document to the driver, or making it available to them, before the job starts.
  • Keeping additional copies where these are needed.
  • Checking the requirements that apply to dangerous goods, ADR, domestic transport, international transport and any special regime.

Mi Carga may add features such as QR codes, download links, date and time stamps, change history, PDF files and cloud storage, in line with the technical setup and the regulations in force.
