2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how Mi Carga processes the personal data of people who:

  • Browse the website.
  • Request information or the trial consignments.
  • Register on the platform.
  • Take out a subscription.
  • Use the app.
  • Contact support.
  • Take part in marketing communications.
  • Are identified in transport documents entered by a customer.

3. Categories of data processed

Depending on your relationship with Mi Carga, the following categories of data may be processed:

3.1. Identification and contact data

  • First name and surname.
  • Company name.
  • Tax ID or tax details.
  • Address.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Business contact details.

3.2. Account and sign-in data

  • Username.
  • Sign-in email address.
  • Encrypted or protected password.
  • Sign-in history.
  • Account status.
  • Configuration preferences.

3.3. Billing and contract data

  • Plan taken out.
  • History of orders and subscriptions.
  • Data needed to issue invoices.
  • Information about payments and renewals.
  • Transaction identifiers.

Mi Carga does not intend to store full bank card details directly where payment is handled through a specialist provider. In that case, processing will be carried out in accordance with that payment provider's privacy policy.

3.4. Data included in transport documents

Users may enter data relating to:

  • Loaders and consignees.
  • Haulage companies.
  • Drivers.
  • Vehicles and registration plates.
  • Goods.
  • Loading and unloading addresses.
  • Routes, schedules and stopping places.
  • Operational notes.
  • Information linked to dangerous goods where applicable.

Customers must enter only the data needed for the transport operation and must have sufficient legal grounds to provide that data to Mi Carga.

3.5. Technical and usage data

  • IP address.
  • Date and time of access.
  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Browser.
  • Technical identifiers.
  • Activity logs.
  • Errors and security events.
  • Data needed for the progressive web app to work.

3.6. Location data

Some features relating to routes, rest areas, lorry parks or service stations may require location data.

Location will only be processed when the user turns on or uses a feature that requires it, and in line with the permissions granted on the device or browser.

5. Processing of data entered by customers

Where a company or professional uses Mi Carga to enter data about drivers, customers, loaders, consignees or other people involved in a transport operation, that customer will generally be the controller of that data.

In those cases, Mi Carga acts as a processor to the extent that it processes the data on the customer's instructions in order to provide the service.

The customer must:

  • Inform the people whose data they enter on the platform.
  • Have a valid legal basis.
  • Enter only data that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary.
  • Not enter special categories of data unless strictly necessary and supported by a valid legal basis.
  • Respond to requests to exercise rights that relate to their own processing.
  • Use the platform in accordance with data protection and transport regulations.

Mi Carga may request further information or restrict features where there is an obvious risk to security or to the protection of personal data.

Where necessary, the parties will enter into a data processing agreement under article 28 of the GDPR.

6. Who receives the data

Data may be disclosed or made accessible to:

  • Hosting, storage and cloud service providers.
  • Maintenance, development and technical support providers.
  • Authentication and account management providers.
  • Payment and invoicing providers.
  • Email and communications providers.
  • Customer service and WhatsApp support providers.
  • Professional advisers, where necessary.
  • Public authorities, courts and law enforcement where there is a legal obligation.
  • Third parties authorised by the customer or by the data subject.

Providers acting as processors must be bound by a valid contract or legal mechanism and must process the data on Mi Carga's instructions.

An up-to-date list of providers that may access personal data is kept in Mi Carga's internal records and can be provided on request where appropriate.

7. International transfers

Mi Carga aims to use providers located within the European Economic Area.

Where a provider is outside the European Economic Area, the transfer will rely on a European Commission adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another valid safeguard under the GDPR.

The providers used at any given time are identified in internal documentation and, where necessary, in the information given to users.

8. How long data is kept

Data is kept for the following periods:

  • Enquiry data: for as long as needed to deal with the request and afterwards for the statutory liability periods.
  • Account data: while the account remains active and for the applicable statutory periods after it is closed.
  • Contract and billing data: for the periods required by commercial, tax and fraud prevention rules.
  • Support data: for as long as needed to resolve the query and afterwards for the statutory limitation periods.
  • Transport documents: for as long as needed to meet the applicable legal obligations and, as a minimum, for one year where the rules applying to the document so require.
  • Data used for marketing communications: until the data subject withdraws consent or objects to the processing.
  • Security logs: for as long as needed to prevent, investigate and evidence incidents.

Once the retention periods end, data is securely deleted or anonymised, unless a legal obligation requires it to be kept longer.

9. Security

Mi Carga applies reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, destruction, alteration, unauthorised access or improper disclosure.

Those measures may include:

  • Encrypted communications over HTTPS.
  • Access control.
  • Credential protection.
  • Backups.
  • Activity and security event logging.
  • Separation of permissions.
  • Continuity and recovery measures.
  • Internal incident management procedures.

No system connected to the internet can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their credentials, use secure devices and report any unauthorised access immediately.

10. QR codes and download links

The documents produced may include QR codes or unique links so they can be viewed or downloaded during an inspection.

Users should bear in mind that anyone who obtains a valid link could access the document, to the extent allowed by the technical setup of the service.

For that reason:

  • Unnecessary personal data should not be included.
  • Links should not be shared publicly unless necessary.
  • The customer must inform the people concerned where appropriate.
  • Mi Carga may apply technical measures for control, expiry, revocation or restriction of access where these are compatible with the applicable regulatory requirements.

11. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects may exercise the following rights:

  • Access: find out whether their data is being processed and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of the data where appropriate.
  • Objection: object to certain processing.
  • Restriction: request that processing be restricted in the cases provided for by law.
  • Portability: receive certain data in a structured format and transmit it to another controller.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent at any time.
  • Not to be subject to solely automated decisions: request human intervention where applicable.

Exercising these rights does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

12. How to exercise your rights

To exercise these rights, data subjects should send a request to soporte@micarga.es.

The request must include:

  • First name and surname.
  • The right you wish to exercise.
  • A description of the request.
  • An email address for the reply.
  • Proof of identity where necessary.

If the request is made on behalf of someone else, proof of authority to act must be provided.

Mi Carga will reply within the periods set out in the applicable rules. Where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, the measures provided for in the GDPR may be applied.

13. Complaints to the supervisory authority

If data subjects consider that the processing of their data breaches the rules, they may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency:

  • Website: https://www.aepd.es/
  • Address: Calle Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid.

Before lodging a complaint, you may contact Mi Carga to try to resolve the matter.

14. Data Protection Officer

At present, unless legally required or expressly stated otherwise, Mi Carga has not appointed a data protection officer.

Questions about data protection may be sent to soporte@micarga.es.

15. Children's data

The platform is aimed at haulage companies and professionals. It is not intended for children.

Mi Carga does not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please tell us at soporte@micarga.es.

16. Changes to this policy

Mi Carga may update this policy to reflect legal, technical or functional changes.

The version in force is the one published on the website, showing the date it was last updated.
