From 5 October 2026, paper is no longer valid

The Sustainable Mobility Act 9/2025 and the update to the ROTT make digital administrative control documents mandatory in Spain. Avoid roadside penalties by making the change today.

  • QR code for inspections: Show the QR on your phone. The officer (Guardia Civil) scans it and validates the digital control document there and then.
  • Legal traceability: The app keeps a tamper-proof record of when each consignment note was created, and of every change made to it.
  • Mandatory 12-month retention: All your PDFs are stored securely in the cloud, as the law requires.

We meet the Resolution of 5 June 2026, point by point

The rule that implements the DeCA (BOE-A-2026-12784) sets very specific technical requirements. Here is what it demands and how Mi Carga meets it:

  • Natively digital PDF, 5 MB maximum (sections 2.1 and 2.2) — we build the document from the data, never from a scan. Our PDFs weigh around 0.5 MB.
  • Creation and modification timestamps as metadata (section 2.1) — recorded in the file itself and in the audit trail.
  • QR code with the document's unique URL (section 2.3) — printed on every PDF.
  • The URL downloads the PDF directly (sections 3.3 and 3.4) — scanning the QR downloads the file: no landing pages, no passwords, no buttons, all of which the rule expressly rules out.
  • HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or above (section 3.1) — all traffic is encrypted.
  • A repository available for inspection (section 1.3) — each document is stored as a file, not just as data.
  • Kept for at least a year (section 2.4) — your documents stay downloadable after the job is done.
  • A copy for the driver before the job starts (section 7) — on the phone or printed, always with its QR.

The Ministry provides no official app for issuing the DeCA: the rule sets the requirements that each company's own software must meet. Mi Carga is that software.
