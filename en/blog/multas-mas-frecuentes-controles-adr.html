Fines and penalties

The 5 most common fines at ADR checks, and how to protect your licence and your pocket

In serious cases the penalties can exceed €4,000.

Carrying dangerous goods takes a lot more than arriving on time. An incomplete document, a badly placed plate or the wrong extinguisher can end in a fine, the vehicle being held and hours of delay. These are the 5 most common offences in Spain and what to check to avoid them.

1. Driving without a transport document, or with the wrong details

The costliest mistake: setting off without the document identifying the goods (UN number, proper shipping name, class, quantity, consignor and consignee). The penalty can range from €1,001-1,500 for documentary errors to more than €4,000 where essential details are missing, with the vehicle held. Produce the document before the job starts and check the details match the actual load.

2. Not carrying the instructions in writing

They must be in the cab and in a language the driver understands — "they're at the office" is not enough. Check them before every departure: present, complete, legible and matching the goods being carried.

3. Driving without the right ADR certificate

An expired or missing certificate, or one that doesn't cover the type of goods or the tank: fines of €1,001 to €2,000, plus the vehicle being held. Check the expiry date and that the training matches the goods before assigning the job.

4. Wrong plates, placards or labels

Orange plates must be fitted, visible, clean and matching the goods actually carried. A hidden or illegible plate can be treated as a serious or very serious offence, with penalties of up to €4,001 and the vehicle held.

5. Missing or unusable mandatory equipment

Chocks, warning signs, hi-vis vests, gloves, absorbent material and extinguishers in good condition with their inspection date up to date. An expired extinguisher can be a serious offence on its own. Build a per-vehicle checklist and run through it at the start of every shift.

Digital paperwork helps, but it does not replace ADR: the driver's certificate, the instructions in writing and the equipment are still your responsibility. Full run-through in ADR paperwork: what the Guardia Civil asks for and in vehicle, marking and ADR routes.
