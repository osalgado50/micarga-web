Checklist

The list of documents the Guardia Civil can ask you for in 2026

Save it on your phone and run through it before you set off.

At a roadside check, the Guardia Civil can inspect the documents covering the driver, the vehicle, the load and the transport job. Here is the full checklist, grouped by section.

The administrative control document (DeCA) must be digital from 5 October 2026. If the change is new to you, start with what the DeCA is and who it applies to — here we focus on the full roadside checklist.

1. Driver's paperwork

  • Proof of identity: national ID card, passport or equivalent. On ADR journeys, with a photograph for every crew member.
  • Driving licence valid and correct for the vehicle (C, C+E, C1 as applicable).
  • Driver CPC in force, if you do professional transport subject to that requirement.
  • Driver card and tachograph records correctly set up, with any manual entries made.

2. Vehicle paperwork

  • The vehicle registration document.
  • The technical data sheet or roadworthiness card.
  • A valid roadworthiness test (ITV).
  • Compulsory insurance.
  • Transport authorisation (MDP, MDL or others, depending on the job).
  • Paperwork for the trailer or semi-trailer, if you are pulling one.

3. Paperwork for the load and the job

Domestic transport: the Administrative Control Document (DeCA), digital from October 2026, showing the contracting loader, the actual carrier, origin and destination, the goods, the registration plate and the driver.

International transport: a CMR consignment note or its electronic version, the e-CMR.

4. International transport and cabotage

  • A copy of the IMI posting declaration, where applicable.
  • Evidence of the operations carried out (an e-CMR, for instance).
  • Tachograph data, including the countries worked in.

5. Extra documents for dangerous goods (ADR)

If you carry dangerous goods the list grows: ADR transport document, instructions in writing, the driver's training certificate and, where applicable, the vehicle and tank approval certificates. The full detail is in which ADR documents the Guardia Civil can ask you for.

6. What isn't paperwork, but gets checked too

A check may cover load securing, ADR marking, fire extinguishers, the hi-vis vest and the condition of tyres and lights. Correct paperwork does not make up for a badly secured load.
