Dangerous goods

ADR paperwork in 2026: which papers (or digital files) the Guardia Civil will ask you for

The complete document checklist for carrying dangerous goods.

If you carry dangerous goods, the general consignment note is not enough. At a check, the Guardia Civil can ask for specific paperwork covering the load, the vehicle and the driver. The reference in 2026 is ADR 2025, together with Royal Decree 97/2014 in Spain.

The good news: some of this paperwork can already be handled digitally, provided it is available throughout the journey, is legible and can be printed without delay.

The transport document (formerly the "ADR consignment note")

ADR 2025 officially calls it the transport document. It must accompany the load before the job starts and clearly identify:

  • The UN number (UN 1203 for petrol, for instance).
  • The proper shipping name.
  • The hazard class and packing group, where applicable.
  • The number and type of packages, and the quantity carried.
  • The tunnel restriction code, where applicable.
  • Consignor, consignee and carrier.

Can you carry it on your phone?

Yes. Section 5.4.0.2 of ADR 2025 allows this paperwork to be handled electronically, but the file must meet specific conditions: immediately available, easy to read, clearly identifying the load and the vehicle, with evidential value equivalent to paper, and quick to print on request.

Instructions in writing: the document that stays within reach

They explain what to do in an accident or emergency (section 5.4.3 of ADR). They must be available in the cab, in a language the driver understands. In Spain the safest option is still to carry them on paper as well as a digital backup: in an emergency, paper works with no battery and no signal.

The driver's ADR certificate

This evidences the training required to handle and carry dangerous goods. Not every certificate covers every job: it depends on the type of goods, whether a tank is involved, and the specific class. Always check that it is valid and matches the operation.

Vehicle approval certificate

Required for certain vehicles: tankers and FL, AT, EX/II or EX/III vehicles. If the certificate doesn't match the vehicle or has expired, showing the consignment note alone won't fix it.

Other certificates depending on the job

  • Tank inspection and leakproofness certificates.
  • Approval of packagings, packing and containers.
  • A container packing certificate, if the journey continues by sea (IMDG Code).

Does the DeCA replace the ADR document?

No. The DeCA is a transport control document; the ADR document identifies and describes the dangerous goods. They can both be handled from the same app, but they are separate obligations.

Quick checklist before setting off

  • A complete ADR transport document matching the load.
  • Instructions in writing, available and understandable.
  • A valid ADR certificate for the driver.
  • Photo ID for every crew member.
  • The vehicle approval certificate, where required.
  • Tank, receptacle or packaging certificates, where applicable.
