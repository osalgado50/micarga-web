2. What the service does

Mi Carga provides a digital tool that may allow you to:

  • Produce transport control documents.
  • Produce consignment notes or other transport-related documents.
  • Create natively digital PDF files.
  • Include QR codes or download links.
  • Record dates, times and changes.
  • Store documents in the cloud.
  • View documents on mobile devices.
  • Use route planning features.
  • Look up rest areas, lorry parks and service stations.

The specific features available depend on the plan taken out, the version of the platform and the technical conditions in place at any given time.

11. Correct use of the documents

Customers are responsible for checking each document before using it.

In particular, they must check:

  • The identity and details of the loader.
  • The identity and details of the carrier.
  • The identity of the consignee.
  • The driver's details.
  • The registration plate and vehicle.
  • The nature and quantity of the goods.
  • Dates and times.
  • The loading and unloading place.
  • The route and any restrictions that apply.
  • Specific requirements for ADR or other regulated goods.
  • The QR code and download link.
  • That the document is legible and available.

The fact that a document is produced automatically does not certify that the data is true, nor that the document is sufficient for every administrative, contractual or safety obligation.
